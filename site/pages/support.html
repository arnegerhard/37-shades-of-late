Support

37 Shades of Late shows upcoming departures for a San Francisco Muni line and stop in the app and on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Getting started

  1. Open the app, tap the line at the top and pick a line, direction and stop.
  2. Tap Save to keep it in your favorites. Tap a favorite to show it in the app and on your widgets.
  3. Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, then add the 37 Shades of Late widget.

Questions

Why are some times green? Green times are live predictions with a bus assigned. Grey times are scheduled estimates for trips that don't have a bus yet.

The widget isn't updating. iOS decides how often widgets refresh to save battery. Tap the refresh button on the widget or open the app to update it right away.

The map doesn't show my bus. The map only shows buses that are on the route. A bus waiting at the yard or finishing another trip appears once it starts its run.

How fresh is the data? Predictions come from 511.org and refresh about every 70 seconds. The thin bar along the bottom of the map counts down to the next update.
