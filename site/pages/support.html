37 Shades of Late shows upcoming departures for a transit line and stop in the app and on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. It covers San Francisco (every Muni line and BART), Seattle (King County Metro, Sound Transit and the Seattle Streetcar), New York (every subway line, the Staten Island Railway, PATH and MTA buses) and Boston (the MBTA's subway, buses, commuter rail and ferries), and the ferries in all four.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap Find a stop on the map to try one out, or + next to Favorites to keep it. Tap your line on the map of the city, then the white arrow at your stop (one per direction), then Choose. The app starts in the city it thinks you're closest to; swipe left or right for another.
  2. It's saved in that city's favorites. Tap a favorite to show it on the map, with its buses or trains on the way; pinch to zoom, or drag with two fingers to move it. Hold a favorite to share or delete it, or its handle to drag it into place.
  3. Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, then add the 37 Shades of Late widget. It shows the favorites of the city you're in, in your order.

Questions

Why are some times green? Green times are live predictions for a bus or train that's on its way. Grey times are scheduled estimates for trips that haven't started yet.

The widget isn't updating. iOS decides how often widgets refresh to save battery. Tap the refresh button on the widget or open the app to update it right away.

The map doesn't show my bus. The map only shows buses that are on the route. A bus waiting at the yard or finishing another trip appears once it starts its run. BART and PATH report only arrival times, not where their trains are, so their trains don't show on the map; New York subway trains appear at the station they last passed.

How fresh is the data? Muni predictions come from 511.org and refresh about every 70 seconds; Seattle's and New York's buses come from OneBusAway and MTA Bus Time and refresh every minute; the New York subway, PATH and BART refresh about every 30 seconds, and Boston about every 20. The thin bar along the bottom of the map counts down to the next update.
