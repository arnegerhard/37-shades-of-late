Privacy Policy

37 Shades of Late doesn't collect anything that identifies you. There are no accounts, ads or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

What the app sends

To show departure times, the app asks our server for the predictions at the stop and line you picked. With each request it sends:

  • the stop, line and direction you're looking at;
  • whether the request came from the app, a widget or the map;
  • how many favorites you've saved, and the app version;
  • which cities you've picked to show in the app;
  • how you get around the app: which screens you open and for how long, which buttons you tap, when you put the app away, and on which screen it crashed or froze, if it did;
  • a random install ID that the app generates the first time it runs.

How you get around the app is sent as screen and button names (like "Home" or "Add favorite"), never anything you type, so we can see where the app is confusing or breaks, and fix it. It's kept on your phone until the app's next request and goes along with it.

The install ID isn't connected to your name, Apple ID, device identifiers or any other app, and we can't link it to you. It only lets us count, anonymously, how many people use the app, how often, and which lines and stops are popular, so we can improve it. Deleting the app deletes the ID; reinstalling creates a new one.

Roughly where you are

With the install ID, our server keeps the rough place our hosting provider derives from your internet connection (like "Oakland, CA"), with coordinates rounded to about 10 km, and when the app or a widget last asked. It's the place your connection comes from, typically accurate only to a city or region, not your device's location, which the app never asks for. We keep the latest one only, to see where the app gets used. Your IP address itself isn't stored.

Picking your city

On first launch, the app asks our server which supported city (San Francisco, Seattle, New York, Boston, Chicago, Washington, D.C. or Los Angeles) is closest, and the widgets ask the same at most every 20 minutes, to show the favorites of the city you're in. The server answers from the rough location our hosting provider derives from your internet connection, typically accurate to a city or region. With it comes how far that is from each city, so the Lock Screen widgets can rest while you're far from all of them. It doesn't ask for your device's location, and nothing is stored. You can switch cities anytime in the app.

Watching a stop from the Dynamic Island

When you tap "Watch it from any app" on the live map, the app sends our server that stop, line and direction, when you started watching, and a push token that Apple creates for this one Live Activity. The server uses them to send the updated times to your Dynamic Island and Lock Screen through Apple's push service, and deletes them when the watch ends, after an hour at most. The push token only reaches that Live Activity; it isn't connected to your install ID or anything else. To know how much watching gets used, we count each watch that starts, with its city, line and stop, but not the push token.

This website

The live departure box on 37shadesoflate.com asks our server for the next 37 at Church & 14th St. That request contains no ID, and the site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

What we don't collect

We don't record your device's location, your IP address or anything else about you, and we never share or sell usage statistics. Your favorites and chosen stop stay on your device. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) keeps short-lived request logs to operate the service.

Data sources

San Francisco departure data comes from 511.org (Metropolitan Transportation Commission) and San Francisco Municipal Transportation Agency, and from BART. Seattle data comes from OneBusAway, with schedules and real-time information from King County Metro, Sound Transit and the City of Seattle. New York data comes from the Metropolitan Transportation Authority (subway, Staten Island Railway and MTA Bus Time), the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey (PATH), NYC Ferry and the New York City Department of Transportation (Staten Island Ferry), and NY Waterway. Boston data comes from the Massachusetts Bay Transportation Authority (MBTA). Chicago data comes from the Chicago Transit Authority (CTA), through its Train Tracker and Bus Tracker. Washington, D.C. data comes from the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority (WMATA). Los Angeles data comes from the Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority (LA Metro), through Swiftly. Ferries in San Francisco and Seattle come from Golden Gate Ferry, San Francisco Bay Ferry and Washington State Ferries. 37 Shades of Late is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.
