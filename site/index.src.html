Origin story · how this got wildly out of hand

Version 0: just me and the 37.

One person at Church & 14th, refreshing a website, waiting for the 37 toward the Haight. So they built a widget, purely to be disappointed faster, right on the Lock Screen.

Then: all of San Francisco.

Turns out everyone in this city is waiting for something. The N, the 38R, the F, the existential dread of the K. Every Muni line got its own shades of late, and BART came along under the Bay.

Then: everyone else wanted in.

Each city was sure its bus was the worst in the country. We checked. They're all correct.

You can suffer together in all these places now.

Your favorites follow you from one to the next. Next stop: everywhere, expected shortly after the heat death of the universe, give or take Muni.

The whole point

Your Lock Screen does the waiting.

Raise your phone, see the minutes, put it back. No unlocking, no app, no tapping through menus while the bus drives past.

  • ⭐All your favorites at onceYour lines in the order you choose, each with minutes until the next two buses.

  • 🧭Comes along when you travelLand in New York and your Lock Screen switches to your New York stops. Nowhere near any of your cities? It naps until you're back. No GPS, no location permission.
